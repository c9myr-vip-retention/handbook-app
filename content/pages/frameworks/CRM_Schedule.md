# CRM Schedule
**Category:** Workflow / Planning
**Applies to:** All VIP CS
**Version:** 1.1
**Last updated:** 26 September 2026 (reload moved to the daily batch roster; WhatsApp weeks confirmed by ATL; source: Official Handbook, 13 May 2026)

---

## 1. Objective
The monthly recurring campaign schedule: which campaigns run on which days, including the reload roster and the WhatsApp Channel invitations.

Daily tasks (Grace Period, LTV, Risk Push, Top PNL, Top Deposit) run every day and are not on this calendar. See each SOP.

## 2. Weekly Core
| Day | Campaign | Target | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wednesday | Telegram Transition IM | Players who have not joined Telegram | Weekly. Filter out players already on Telegram before sending |
| Wednesday, Saturday | Telegram Business Outbound Campaign | Per campaign | Weekly |
| Daily, one team per day | Reload: Ang Bao (TC) + Redeem Code (Zyra) | Per the batch roster | Mon to Wed: TC Tier 3 + Zyra Tier V. Thu to Sat: TC Tier V + Zyra Tier 3. Follow the "Reload - Angpao and Redeem code By Batch" roster. See Weekend Campaign SOP |

## 3. Grace Period (weekly)
| Day | List | Player condition | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | Grace Period Player list | Has not reached the monthly deposit threshold | Grace Period IM + Push; eligible list to Admin for SMS |
| Tuesday | Potential Player list | Has reached or passed the threshold | Potential Player IM + Push; eligible list to Admin for SMS |
| Thursday | Follow-up: Monday list | Monday players who did not respond or convert | Follow-up IM + Push |
| Friday | Follow-up: Tuesday list | Tuesday players who did not respond or convert | Follow-up IM + Push |

## 4. WhatsApp Channel Invitations
Biweekly: two invitation IMs a month, on the **1st and 3rd Thursday**, to all eligible Tier 3 / Tier V players. The second one follows up on players who did not join after the first.

## 5. Remarks
- Reload tier targeting follows the roster days; never swap Tier 3 and Tier V.
- Free Spin on Zyra was discontinued in September 2026 and replaced by Redeem Code.
