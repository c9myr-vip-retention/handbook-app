# Engagement Strategy (Tier 3 / Tier V)
**Category:** Workflow / Strategy
**Applies to:** All VIP CS
**Version:** 1.0
**Last updated:** 13 May 2026 (source: Official Handbook)

---

## 1. Objectives
- Define every Tier 3 / Tier V sub-segment and make sure each is covered by an operational strategy during prime time.
- Use segmentation and behaviour triggers so every player has an engagement action at every lifecycle stage, with no missed touchpoints.
- Use milestone-based, rule-based rewards to remove inconsistency from manual judgment.

This page is the strategic overview. Step-by-step execution is in each SOP.

## 2. Core Principles: Secret Wallet Control
- The Secret Wallet (SW) is the core control. Every reward is tied to SW and has a behavioural basis.
- Where SW is not supported, only basic chat engagement runs. No reward is triggered.
- Follow the process in order. No skipped steps, nothing run in parallel.
- One owner per player at each stage. No duplicate contact.
- Every plan has a fixed target number of players. Do not go over or under.
- SW x 40% is allocated to engagement rewards; the remaining 60% can go to other schemes in rotation.
- Players with a history of profit get no SW reward, even if they are losing this cycle. Basic engagement only.

## 5. Performance Targets
| KPI | Formula | Target | Bands |
|---|---|---|---|
| Live Chat conversion rate | Players who entered Live Chat / players contacted | 30% or more | 30%+ good; 20 to 29% improve; under 20% failed |
| Post-bonus deposit recovery | Players who deposited within 3 days / players given a bonus | 50% or more | 50%+ strong; 30 to 49% acceptable; under 30% poor |
| Bonus ROI | Total deposit generated / total bonus given | 3.0x or more | 3.0+ healthy; 2.0 to 2.9 monitor; under 2.0 over-spending |
| Execution discipline | Random audit of chats, bonus records and tagging | 100% | 100% pass; any violation fails |

- Live Chat conversion baseline was 15% (tested). Every campaign needs a CTA to VIP Live Chat.
- Track deposits on Day 1, 2 and 3 after the bonus. Suggested add-on: deposit return of at least 3x the bonus.
- Bonus ROI is tracked in the ROI Table (see the ROI Table SOP).
- Execution discipline is measured through the QA Record and the Mistake Log.

## 6. Remarks
- SW x 40% applies to Top PNL, LTV and Livechat complaints. SW x 20% applies to Grace Period only.
- SMS is always sent by the Retention Team Admin. VIP CS prepares the eligible list only.
- Every reward needs a Lark ticket with the correct query. Tick Claimed after submitting.
