# KPI Framework
**Category:** Performance / Framework
**Applies to:** All VIP RTN staff; ATL (scorer)
**Version:** 2.0
**Last updated:** 26 September 2026 (general framework; personalised monthly metrics confirmed by ATL; replaces the May 2026 team KPI tables)

---

## 1. How KPI Works
- Every staff member has a **monthly KPI scorecard**, scored out of 100.
- The ATL sets each person's metrics and weights **at the start of the month**. They are personalised: they target that person's weakest areas so the score drives real improvement.
- The scorecard is shared with the staff member before the month starts, so they know what they are measured on.
- At month-end the ATL scores each metric from evidence, using the KPI Scoring SOP.

## 2. Scoring
- Each metric is scored on the band scale: **0, 40, 60, 80 or 100**.
- Weighted score = metric score x weight. The total is the sum of the weighted scores.
- A raw result that lands exactly on a threshold takes the band it meets, not the higher one.

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 60 to 65 | No mistakes; baseline |
| 65 to 70 | Slight improvement; still needs pointers |
| 70 to 80 | Improving on weaknesses; fewer pointers |
| 80 to 90 | Above expectation; self-improving |
| 90 to 100 | Near perfect; exceeds the role |

## 3. Rules
- **Every score is backed by evidence.** No invented figures.
- **Post-close data only.** Same-day figures change as the day fills and are not scored.
- **Never edit a score to hit a target.** If the result feels wrong, fix the metric or weight for next month, not the cell. Lowering a correct score is falsifying a record.
- New staff on probation are scored for coaching and evaluation only; incentives start after probation (see New Staff Incentive Eligibility SOP).

## 4. Common Metric Areas
The ATL picks each month's metrics from areas like these:
- Livechat response time (FRT)
- Discipline (mistakes and SOP compliance, from the Mistake Log and QA Record)
- Attendance and punctuality
- Reactivation (contact must come before the deposit)
- Deposit growth and retention
- Leadership and team support (Seniors)
- Escalation, reporting and handover quality

## 5. Remarks
- See the KPI Scoring SOP for the step-by-step scoring process.
- Team-level targets (retention, deposit growth) are set by management and may change month to month.
