# Livechat Handling Framework
**Category:** Soft Skill / Chat
**Applies to:** All VIP CS
**Version:** 1.0
**Last updated:** 13 May 2026 (source: Official Handbook)

---

## 1. Objective
The structured approach every CS applies when serving a VIP player on Livechat.

## 2. The 4 Pillars
| Pillar | Principle |
|---|---|
| Communication | Friendly, natural Livechat style that builds rapport |
| Bonus Management | Strategic use of Angpao, Free Credit and Reload offers |
| Retention | Soft skills that keep VIP players engaged and loyal |
| Complaints | Smart, empathetic handling of requests and issues |

## 3. First Steps in Every Chat
1. **Ask for the UID or username.** Always start here, even with returning players. Sample: "Hi boss, boleh bagi UID atau username? Saya check account boss dulu."
2. **Pull up the player profile.** Open the full account in the back office before writing any reply.
3. **Assess before you respond.** Use what you find to choose the approach, tone and offer.
4. **Check the player's P&L.** Whether they are up or down shapes how you empathise and what you offer.

## 4. What to Check in the Player Profile
| Check | What it tells you | How it affects your reply |
|---|---|---|
| VIP tier (T3 or TV) | Which bonuses and credit amounts apply | T3 = 3% Secret Wallet (Grace Period capped at SW x 20%). TV = 5% Secret Wallet, eligible for higher Free Credit / Angpao tiers |
| Last deposit time | Whether the player has gone quiet | Recent deposit = active; long gap = retention concern. Shapes reload and re-engagement offers |
| Weekly deposit and loss | How much they deposited and lost this week | Drives bonus eligibility and the Angpao / Free Credit / Reload decision |
| Player P&L (win / loss) | Whether they are up or down overall | Shapes empathy and the offer |

## 5. Remarks
- VIP players drive high deposits and long-term value. Good CS communication raises deposits, improves retention, cuts complaints and builds lasting relationships.
- CS is not just support. CS is a retention engine.
- Chinese-speaking VIPs with no Chinese-speaking CS free: use ChatGPT to translate, or politely ask whether English or Malay is fine. Transfer complex cases to a Chinese-speaking CS when one is available.
