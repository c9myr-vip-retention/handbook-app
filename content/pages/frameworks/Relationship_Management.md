# Relationship Management
**Category:** Soft Skill / Engagement
**Applies to:** All VIP CS
**Version:** 1.0
**Last updated:** 13 May 2026 (source: Official Handbook)

---

## 1. Objective
Build long-term VIP relationships through everyday conversation, empathy and well-timed engagement, beyond transactional support.

## 2. When to Build the Relationship
| Moment | Why it works |
|---|---|
| Just finished playing | They are winding down: a good time for a casual chat |
| Waiting / idle | They have time and headspace to talk |
| Neutral mood | No complaint, no urgency: an open window |
| Not asking for a bonus | Reach out just to connect, with no agenda |

Relationships are built in the quiet moments, not only in the crisis ones.

## 3. Conversation Topics That Work
| Topic | Sample opener | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Food | "Boss makan apa hari ni?" / "Boss suka makan pedas ke?" | Universal, safe, warm |
| Weekend plans | "Boss weekend ada plan?" | Light and relatable |
| Holiday / travel | "Boss ada plan pergi mana cuti ni?" | Shows genuine interest in their life |
| Local lifestyle | "Boss cuti sekolah ada jalan mana mana?" / "Kalau free time boss suka buat apa?" | Local and personal |
| Games (talk like a player) | "Boss tadi main game apa?" / "Game tu kadang keras sikit" | Shared ground |
| Jackpot talk | "Boss game ni macam nak drop je" / "Ramai tengah hit jackpot sekarang" | Builds excitement and curiosity |

## 4. Handling Emotion with Empathy
| Step | Approach | Example | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Empathise first | Acknowledge the feeling directly and sincerely | "Faham boss, memang frust kalau kalah." | Defuses most tension at once |
| 2. Gently redirect | Offer a soft, low-pressure alternative | "Boleh try game lain dulu boss." | Repositions the moment without dismissing it |

## 5. Making VIPs Feel Special
- "Boss account boss memang saya jaga."
- "Kalau ada special saya inform boss dulu."
- Use the player's name ("Boss [Name]") consistently, never "Member".
- Refer back to what you remember: favourite game, last topic, preferences.
- Offer first. "I noticed X, would you like Y?" beats "How can I help?"
- Closing line, every time: "Next time sebelum main boleh cari saya ya boss."

## 6. Do's and Don'ts
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Use the player's name | Say "Member" or "Customer" |
| Refer to past conversations | Treat every chat like the first |
| Lead with empathy when they are losing | Jump into bonus or sales mode |
| Plant a personal connection when closing | End with "anything else?" and leave |
| Use casual Malay where it fits ("boss", "jap") | Sound stiff or robotic |
| One follow-up after silence, then wait | Spam follow-ups |
| Recommend games that match their taste | Push the same "hot" games to everyone |
| Offer first | Wait for them to ask |

## 7. Remarks
- Relationship management is not a separate task. It is the tone of every interaction.
- A VIP who feels like a friend is far less likely to leave.
