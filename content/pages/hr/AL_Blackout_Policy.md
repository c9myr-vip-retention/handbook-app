# AL Blackout Policy
**Category:** HR / Policy
**Applies to:** All VIP RTN staff
**Version:** 1.1
**Last updated:** 25 September 2026 (approval rule confirmed by ATL; source: Official Handbook, 13 May 2026)

---

## 1. Objective
Restrict leave in peak periods so there is enough manpower during high-volume windows.

## 2. Purpose
Keep coverage through peak operational periods, especially between month-end and month-start, when deposit and withdrawal volumes are high and PG or banking instability is more likely.

## 3. Policy
| Provision | Detail |
|---|---|
| Rest day (RD) change requests | Judged on manpower availability. Generally discouraged, to avoid staffing gaps |
| Blackout window | No fixed dates. AL in peak periods (around month-end and month-start) needs approval from the ATL or above |
| Exceptions | Emergency or critical leave only, with approval from the ATL or above and supporting reasons submitted as early as possible |
| Compliance | Everyone must follow this policy. Non-compliance may disrupt operations and lead to disciplinary action |
