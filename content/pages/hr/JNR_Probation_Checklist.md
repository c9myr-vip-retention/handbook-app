# JNR Probation Checklist
**Category:** HR / Evaluation
**Applies to:** Senior and ATL (evaluators); new JNR VIP RTN staff (evaluated)
**Version:** 1.1
**Last updated:** 25 September 2026 (probation length confirmed by ATL; source: Official Handbook, 28 May 2026)

---

## 1. Purpose
Used by the Senior and ATL to evaluate new JNR staff at the end of probation. Standard probation is **3 months**. Outcome: **Confirmed Junior**, **Probation Extended** (standard not yet met), or **Not Confirmed**. The fillable scorecard lives in the Probation Evaluation Checklist; this page is the reference.

## 2. Section A: Basic Task Execution
Source: Senior observation across the probation period. **Pass: all 4.**

| No. | Criterion | Pass condition | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Livechat handling | Replies within the FRT standard. No unattended chats; no closing without resolution | Senior observation |
| A2 | Tagging accuracy | Tags every chat before closing. No pattern of missing, wrong or double tags | Senior + tag audit |
| A3 | SOP adherence | Follows the SOP step by step when guided. No skipped steps or improvising without confirmation | Senior observation |
| A4 | Task submission | Submits QA Record and Output Report before shift end without repeated reminders | QA Record + Output Report |

## 3. Section B: Learning and Improvement
Source: Mistake Log and Senior observation. **Pass: 3 of 4.**

| No. | Criterion | Pass condition | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | Mistake reduction | The same mistake is not repeated more than twice after correction | Mistake Log |
| B2 | Asking when unsure | Asks instead of guessing or proceeding wrongly | Senior observation |
| B3 | Response to feedback | Accepts correction without being defensive, and applies it | Senior observation |
| B4 | Visible improvement | Measurable improvement from the first month to the last in at least one area (FRT, tagging, submission, SOP accuracy) | Mistake Log + QA Record |

## 4. Section C: Professionalism and Reliability
Source: attendance records, Insightful, team observation. **Pass: 3 of 4, and C1 must pass** (failing C1 is an automatic fail).

| No. | Criterion | Pass condition | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Attendance and punctuality (non-negotiable) | Consistent attendance. No unexplained absence, repeated late logins or disappearing mid-shift | Insightful + attendance log |
| C2 | Team communication | Tells the team before breaks, updates on task completion, never goes silent on shift | Discord + team channel |
| C3 | Customer conduct | Professional, composed tone. No recorded complaints or attitude issues | QA Record + Livechat audit |
| C4 | Security compliance | Uses VPN and Incognito. Never shares BO, Livechat or system access with unauthorised people | Precaution & Security check |

## 5. Section D: Automatic Fail
Any one of these means **Not Confirmed**, whatever the other scores.

| No. | Condition | Description | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Unauthorised bonus | Issued, or tried to issue, a bonus without Senior or ATL approval | BO log + bonus audit |
| D2 | Account security breach | Shared BO, Livechat or any system access with an unauthorised person | Security incident report |
| D3 | Repeated no-show | Absent without documents or approval more than once during probation | Attendance log |
| D4 | Conduct violation | Aggressive, disrespectful or inappropriate conduct toward a customer or colleague, on record | Incident log + QA audit |

## 6. Outcome
| Section | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| A: Basic Task Execution | All 4 |
| B: Learning and Improvement | 3 of 4 |
| C: Professionalism and Reliability | 3 of 4, C1 required |
| D: Automatic Fail | None triggered |

All sections passed and no automatic fail = **Confirmed Junior VIP RTN**. If the Junior is close but not yet at standard, probation may be extended instead of Not Confirmed.

Who signs: the Senior fills A, B and C; the ATL fills D and makes the decision; the Supervisor gives the overall recommendation.
