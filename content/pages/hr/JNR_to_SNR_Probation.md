# JNR to SNR Probation
**Category:** HR / Evaluation
**Applies to:** ATL (evaluator); confirmed JNR VIP RTN staff (evaluated)
**Version:** 1.0
**Last updated:** 17 May 2026 (source: Official Handbook)

---

## 1. Purpose
Used by the ATL to evaluate a confirmed Junior for promotion to Senior. Outcome: **Confirmed Senior** or **Remain Junior**. Pass mark: **75% overall (16 of 21 criteria)**. The fillable scorecard lives in the Probation Evaluation Checklist; this page is the reference.

## 2. Section A: Knowledge Assessment
Can the candidate answer without the Handbook? Verbal or written, assessed by the ATL.

| No. | Question | Pass condition |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Grace Period: Player vs Potential Player | Explains the difference and which day each is run |
| A2 | Risk Push tier logic | Explains the channels for 1D inactivity, and for 3D and above |
| A3 | Top PNL eligibility | States the conditions a player must pass before a bonus is issued |
| A4 | Escalation path | States when to escalate a case, and to whom |
| A5 | Tagging rules | Defines wrong tag, miss tag and double tag, and the consequence of each |

## 3. Section B: Chat Quality (last 14 days)
Source: audit of the last 14 days of Livechat records.

| No. | Criterion | Pass condition | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | FRT average | Consistently under 10 seconds | Livechat report |
| B2 | Tag accuracy | Zero wrong, missing or double tags | Tag audit |
| B3 | Chat closure | Always offers further help before closing | Livechat transcript |
| B4 | Bonus handling | No incorrect or unauthorised bonus issuance | Bonus log + BO audit |
| B5 | Escalation timing | Escalates promptly; never sits on difficult cases | Escalation log + transcripts |
| B6 | Customer tone | Professional and composed, including with difficult players | Transcript review |

## 4. Section C: SOP Execution (last 14 days)
Source: QA Records and Output Reports.

| No. | Criterion | Pass condition | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Task completion | All assigned tasks done on time, none missed | Output Report |
| C2 | QA screenshots | Attached for every task without reminders | QA Record |
| C3 | Output Report | Submitted before shift end, consistently | Output Report timestamps |
| C4 | Repeat mistakes | None recorded in the Mistake Log | Mistake Log |
| C5 | Mistake severity | Overall count and severity within an acceptable range | Mistake Log review |

## 5. Section D: Seniority Behaviour
Source: evidence across the whole probation, beyond task execution.

| No. | Criterion | Pass condition | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Proactive support | Has helped a junior without being asked | ATL + Senior observation |
| D2 | Process initiative | Has flagged a process gap, mistake or issue to the ATL or TL unprompted | ATL log / chat history |
| D3 | Team communication | Updates the team clearly; does not work silently | Discord / team channel |
| D4 | Composure under pressure | Stays calm with pressure and difficult customers | Transcript + observation |
| D5 | Shift independence | Has held a Livechat shift alone without ATL intervention | ATL observation |

## 6. Outcome
| Section | Criteria |
|---|---|
| A: Knowledge Assessment | 5 |
| B: Chat Quality | 6 |
| C: SOP Execution | 5 |
| D: Seniority Behaviour | 5 |
| **Total** | **21 (pass = 16 or more, 75%)** |

75% and above = **Confirmed Senior VIP RTN**. Below 75% = Remain Junior; re-evaluate next cycle.

Who signs: the ATL leads the evaluation; the Supervisor signs off the final outcome.
