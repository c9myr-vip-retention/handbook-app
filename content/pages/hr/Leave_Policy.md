# Leave Policy
**Category:** HR / Policy
**Applies to:** All VIP RTN staff
**Version:** 1.1
**Last updated:** 25 September 2026 (accrual corrected by ATL; source: Official Handbook, 13 May 2026)

---

## 1. Objective
Define entitlements, request procedures and conditions for every leave type.

## 2. Entitlements
| Leave type | Entitlement | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Annual Leave (AL) | 24 days per year | Up to 5 days carry over; the rest is paid out |
| Sick Leave (SL) | 7 days per year | Does not carry over |
| Paternity Leave (PL) | 3 days per year | Request 3 days before the expected birth date |
| Maternity Leave (ML) | 60 days per year | Notify HR, supervisor and TL 2 months before the expected delivery date |

## 3. Annual Leave
- Request AL at least 14 days in advance.
- Use your leave within the same calendar year where possible.
- Up to 5 days carry over to the next year; any remaining unused days are paid out.
- Submit requests through the company leave system or via Lark to your supervisor.
- Your supervisor reviews and approves requests.
- When operations are critical, leave may be denied or moved during peak periods (see AL Blackout Policy).

## 4. Sick Leave
- Tell your supervisor at least 1 hour before shift start.
- A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 3 consecutive days.
- Unused sick leave does not carry over.
- See Work Policy Annex D for sick leave that counts as a violation.

## 5. Paternity Leave
- For fathers supporting their partner at the birth of a child.
- Request at least 3 days before the expected birth date.
- Paid, in line with local labour law.
- Unused paternity leave does not carry over.

## 6. Maternity Leave
- Notify HR, your supervisor and TL 2 months before the expected delivery date.
- Pay: 100% of salary in the first month, 50% in the second month.
- Return to work after the 60 days unless extra leave is approved.

## 7. Unauthorised Absence
- **No-show:** not reporting for work without notice or approval is unauthorised absence and may lead to disciplinary action. Recorded as UPL in the roster.
- **Unapproved sick leave:** sick leave without proper notice or a medical certificate may be marked unauthorised.
- Repeated unapproved sick leave may lead to disciplinary action and affect future leave approval.

## 8. Accrual and Balance
| Provision | Detail |
|---|---|
| Accrual | AL accrues monthly: 2 days per month (24 days / 12 months) |
| Balance | Track your balance in the company leave system |
| Use | Take leave through the year to prevent burnout |

## 9. Special Considerations
- **Peak times:** avoid leave during peak business periods unless it is an emergency. Requests in busy periods may be denied to keep enough staff.
- **Extended leave:** leave over 30 days needs a detailed request at least 3 weeks in advance, subject to operational needs.
