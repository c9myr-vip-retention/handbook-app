# Precaution & Security
**Category:** HR / Policy
**Applies to:** All VIP RTN staff
**Version:** 1.2
**Last updated:** 28 September 2026 (Handbook App access rules added, approved by ATL; previous: 25 September 2026, VPN servers updated by ATL; source: Official Handbook, 13 May 2026)

---

## 1. Objective
Mandatory safety, communication and account-handling rules for all VIP RTN staff.

## 2. Safety Precautions
- Never take work outside, at any time.
- Short power cut: use a mobile data hotspot until power returns.
- Long power cut: reschedule your day off or apply for leave.
- Always log out of Line OA, Livechat and the BO after work, or when leaving your workstation for a long time.
- Always use Incognito mode, so no history is stored on your device (see the Incognito Browsing SOP).
- Always use the VPN during work (NordLayer, server M01, M02 or M03).
- Never save work on your laptop. All work goes through the drive or Lark sheets.
- Do not tell friends or family what you do. Protect yourself.

## 3. Communication and Security Rules
| Topic | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| External contacts on Lark | Recognise external accounts (clearly marked). Do not accept or reply to any external contact, especially impersonators with similar names | Impersonators try to breach our systems |
| Suspicious Telegram messages | Be careful with new Telegram accounts sending private messages. Do not reply; report to your direct superior at once | Scammers use new accounts. Not reporting = formal warning letter |
| Official channels | All work matters are discussed in official group chats only. Private messages for work are strictly prohibited | Group chats give transparency and supervision. Violation = warning letter or termination |
| Bank app / OTP requests | No one may ask for bank app authorisation, OTP codes or financial approvals by private message | A common scam and data-breach tactic |
| BO access / password resets | Any BO access or reset request goes through official group channels and the proper procedure | Prevents unauthorised access; keeps change control |
| Authorised group chats | All work chat happens in official groups monitored by the HOD. Side groups without the HOD are not allowed and must be closed | Transparency; lets the HOD oversee operations |

## 4. Handbook App Access
- Sign in to the Handbook App with your company email. A personal email is allowed only if it is registered with your ATL first.
- The first sign-in with a personal email waits for admin approval. Do not share or forward your sign-in with anyone.
- Use the Handbook App as the only current version of SOPs and policies. The old HANDBOOK(new) workbook is kept for reference only.
- Do not copy, screenshot or share Handbook content outside the department.
- Access is removed the same day a staff member resigns, is terminated, or moves out of the department.
- Report lost access, or a login you do not recognise, to your ATL at once.

## 5. Remarks
- Breaking any communication or security rule may lead to immediate disciplinary action, up to termination.
- All staff complete a security awareness acknowledgement at onboarding and every year after.
