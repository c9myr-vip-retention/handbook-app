# Resignation Policy
**Category:** HR / Policy
**Applies to:** All VIP RTN staff
**Version:** 1.0
**Last updated:** 13 May 2026 (source: Official Handbook)

---

## 1. Objective
Standardise resignation and notice for the CS team, so handovers are smooth, operations are not disrupted, and everyone knows the rules.

## 2. Scope
- Staff understand their resignation responsibilities.
- Management can plan workforce continuity.
- A fair, transparent process avoids misunderstandings.
- Applies to all C9MYR VIP RTN team members.

## 3. Notice Period
| Staff type | Notice required |
|---|---|
| Confirmed staff | At least 1 month |
| Staff on probation | At least 7 days |

## 4. Resigning Without Proper Notice (Confirmed Staff)
| Notice given | Final salary | KPI bonus / revenue share | Other entitlements |
|---|---|---|---|
| 30 days (full notice) | Paid in full (0% forfeited) | Eligible | Night shift allowance and annual leave honoured |
| Less than 30 days | 100% forfeited | Not eligible | Not eligible |

## 5. Remarks
- Follow the proper procedure to keep your full entitlements and hand over smoothly.
- Probation staff who resign without proper notice: final entitlements are at management's discretion.
- All knowledge transfer, account handovers and pending tasks must be completed and signed off before the last working day.
