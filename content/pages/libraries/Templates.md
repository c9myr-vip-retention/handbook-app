## 1. Objective
Reusable Livechat reply templates in Malay, English and Chinese. Use them as a starting point and personalise to the player.

## 2. Livechat Templates
| Type | Malay (BM) | English (EN) | Chinese (CN) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Greeting | Terima kasih kerana menghubungi Pasukan Sokongan VIP [brand], di mana sokongan istimewa disediakan untuk ahli kami yang paling berprestij. | Thank you for reaching out to the VIP Support Team, where dedicated support is reserved for our most prestigious members. | 感谢您联系 VIP 客服团队，这里为我们最尊贵的会员提供专属支持。 |
| Holding | Jap ye boss, saya check account boss dulu. | One moment please, let me check your account first. | 老板请稍后一下下哟。 |
| Closing | Terima kasih kerana menghubungi kami, boss. Semoga bernasib baik dan selamat bermain! 🍀 | Thank you for reaching out, boss. Wishing you the best of luck and happy playing! 🍀 | 感谢您联系我们，老板。祝您好运，玩得开心！🍀 |
| Best wishes | Semoga boss menang besar hari ni! Kalau perlukan apa-apa lagi, jangan segan untuk hubungi kami semula ya. 😊 | Hope you have a big win today! Feel free to reach out again if you need anything. 😊 | 希望老板今天大赢！如有任何需要，随时联系我们。😊 |
| Get username | Boleh tolong berikan username akaun boss? Saya nak semak untuk boss. 🙏 | Could you please provide your account username? I'll look into it for you. 🙏 | 请问可以提供您的账号用户名吗？我帮您查看一下。🙏 |
| Get UID | Boleh tolong berikan UID akaun boss? Saya nak semak untuk boss. 🙏 | Could you please provide your account UID? I'll look into it for you. 🙏 | 请问可以提供您的账号UID吗？我帮您查看一下。🙏 |

- Greeting: replace [brand] with the brand name.
- Holding: send it right after you get the UID or username, so the player knows you are checking.

- Always use the latest template from the Team Engagement File.
- Do not change a template without your TL's approval.
- If a template is missing or out of date, tell your ATL or TL.
