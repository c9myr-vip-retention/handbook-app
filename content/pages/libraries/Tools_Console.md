## 1. Objective
The consoles the team uses: what each does, who uses it, and where it is.

## 2. Consoles in Use
| Console | What it does | Users | Owner | Address |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| VVIP Console (Tracker) | VVIP activity tracker. Shows each day who to contact, who to leave alone, and how. Tabs: Dashboard, Daily (with the Assigned list), Changes, Campaigns, Performance, Settings | VIP CS + ATL | VIP RTN | https://groupb-tracker.pages.dev/ |
| iMessage Console | Sends Internal Mail without the Back Office. Recipient lists built by formula from the BO; content per brand; HTML upload; scheduled sends | VIP CS + ATL | VIP RTN | https://imassage.96ghq.com/ |
| Telegram Console | Outbound Telegram broadcast through per-brand bots, no ad budget. GENERAL and VVIP bots kept separate; scheduling, bulk send, full audit log | VIP CS + ATL | VIP RTN | https://myrtelegrambot.96ghq.com/ |
| C9 HR Portal | Attendance, leave requests and approval, lateness, waivers and leave balance. Staff see My Workspace; ATL and above see the admin pages | All staff | HR | https://hr-portal.96ghq.com/ |

## 3. Remarks
- The VVIP Console address can change. Confirm the current link with your ATL. Its walkthrough is the Agent Handbook v2.0 (effective 3 Sep 2026).
- The HR Portal is owned by HR. VIP RTN references it but does not maintain it. The iMessage and Telegram consoles are VIP RTN tools.
- Bot tokens, passwords and staff records inside these consoles are sensitive. Never copy them into the Handbook or share them.
