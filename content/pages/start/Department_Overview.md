## 1. Mission
The VIP Retention department drives long-term player value through structured outreach, personalised engagement and disciplined performance tracking. We retain Tier 3 and Tier V players by combining a systematic multi-channel approach (IM, Push, SMS, Livechat, Telegram) with high-quality service.

## 4. Key Roles
| Role | Function | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Department Head | Department oversight | Strategic decisions and KPI ownership |
| Supervisor | Operational supervision | Day-to-day team performance and escalations |
| Assistant Team Leader (ATL) | Team leadership | Each ATL leads one team (A, B or C) |
| Senior VIP RTN | Senior outreach and mentoring | Complex cases, mentoring Juniors |
| Junior VIP RTN | Daily outreach | Runs the SOPs each shift |
| Retention Team (Admin) | Data provisioning | Provides the daily player data and sends SMS on behalf of VIP RTN |

## 5. Remarks
- The Retention Team is the Admin function of the VIP Retention department, not a separate department. They provide the data; VIP RTN runs the outreach.
- All outreach is tracked in the QA Record (see the QA Record SOP).
- Performance is measured under the KPI Framework.
