## 1. Shifts
All times are GMT+8.

| Shift | Hours | Break |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | 07:00 to 19:00 | 2 hours, per the break schedule |
| Mid | 16:00 to 04:00 | 2 hours, per the break schedule |
| Night | 19:00 to 07:00 | 2 hours, per the break schedule |

- Your shift and rest days are in the Roster.
- **Breaks are scheduled (from September 2026).** Each person has fixed break slots for the week in the Roster's break schedule. Take your break only in your slot.
- Start and end every break on Insightful (Workpuls). Excessive break time is a Work Policy offence.

## 2. Before Every Shift
1. Pre-shift setup: VPN, clock in on Insightful, Chrome in Incognito (see Attendance SOP).
2. Channel login (see Channel Login SOP).
3. Check the Handover tab for items left by the previous shift (see Shift Handover SOP).

## 6. Every Shift
- **Livechat:** handle chats within the FRT standard and tag every chat (see Tag Library).
- **QA Record:** update right after each task, not at the end of the shift.
- **Output Report:** submit before the shift ends.
- **Handover:** log open items for the next shift in the last 15 minutes.

## 7. Remarks
- Telegram Approach runs between other tasks, following the priority order in its SOP.
- If a task cannot be finished in your shift, record it as pending in the Output Report and the Handover tab, and tell your ATL before you leave.
