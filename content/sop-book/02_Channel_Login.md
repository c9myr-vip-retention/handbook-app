# SOP: Channel Login
**Document Code:** C9MYR-RTN-SOP-O01
**Category:** Operation / All Shifts
**Applies to:** All VIP CS
**Owner:** VIP RTN | **Review cycle:** Quarterly or on material change | **Version:** 1.1 (4-element retrofit 7 Sep 2026)
**Last updated:** 7 September 2026 (4-element retrofit; source: Official Handbook, 13 May 2026)

---

## 1. Objective
Ensure all working channels are active and properly set up before the shift begins.

## 2. Scope
Applies to every agent at the start and end of every shift, covering both Discord and LiveChat.

## 3. Golden Rules
- Log in to both Discord and LiveChat at least 10 minutes before shift start, to avoid late attendance records.
- Never leave LiveChat unattended during active hours. Communicate all breaks in advance.
- Report any Discord or LiveChat issue to the TL or ATL at once, and document it for attendance.

## 4. Procedure: Discord (Action / Sequence / Degree / Tool)
| # | Action | Sequence (when) | Degree / standard | Tool |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Log in to Discord | Before shift start | Use assigned credentials; contact ATL/TL on login issues | Discord |
| 2 | New joiners: verify in the #rules channel with the V button | Once, on first joining | One time only | Discord |
| 3 | Go to the VIP Retention Tier 3 and V channel | When on duty | Stay there through the shift | Discord |
| 4 | Go to the Training Room channel | For training | As needed | Discord |
| 5 | Go to the Meeting Room channel | For a meeting | As needed | Discord |
| 6 | Move to AFK and tell the team in chat | Short breaks under 10 min | Toilet or smoke breaks only | Discord |
| 7 | Move to the Lunch/Dinner Break channel | Meal breaks up to 1 hour | Inform the team first | Discord |
| 8 | Notify the team and move to AFK | Before shift end | Complete or hand over pending tasks | Discord |
| 9 | Log out of Discord | At shift end | Clean logout | Discord |

## 5. Procedure: LiveChat (Action / Sequence / Degree / Tool)
| # | Action | Sequence (when) | Degree / standard | Tool |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Log in to LiveChat | At least 10 min before shift (7:00 AM or 7:00 PM) | Early login gives time to fix connection issues | LiveChat |
| 2 | Verify internet and the LiveChat system work | After login | Refresh or run a speed check if needed | LiveChat |
| 3 | Set agent status correctly | Before accepting chats | Status must reflect availability | LiveChat |
| 4 | Click Accept Chats | 1 to 2 minutes before shift start | Prevents delays | LiveChat |
| 5 | Go to Chats and begin serving customers | At shift start | Serve in order | LiveChat |
| 6 | Reply politely and professionally | Throughout the shift | Warm, helpful, VIP-standard tone | LiveChat |
| 7 | Verify message, promotion and brand before replying | Before each reply | Double-check brand-specific promos and terms | LiveChat |
| 8 | Seek help from Senior, ATL or TL | Whenever unsure | Do not guess or delay, escalate | LiveChat / Lark |
| 9 | Do not leave a chat unattended without telling the team | During active hours | Transfer or assign before stepping away | LiveChat |
| 10 | Stop new chats and finish active ones | Before shift end | Allow 5 to 10 min to wrap up | LiveChat |
| 11 | Set status to Offline | At shift end | Clean status change | LiveChat |
| 12 | Ensure all chats are closed or handed over | Before logout | Escalate unresolved chats to the next shift | LiveChat |

## 6. Guards
- If Discord or LiveChat is inaccessible at shift start, inform the TL immediately and document it for attendance.
- No chat left unattended during active hours without a handover.
- A late login risks a late attendance record; the 10-minute buffer is the guard.

## 7. Remarks
- Report any LiveChat system issue to the TL or ATL at once, to prevent attendance discrepancies.
- Escalate unresolved chats to the next shift rather than closing them unfinished.
