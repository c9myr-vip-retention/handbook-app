## 1. Objective
Notify newly upgraded VVIP players via Internal Mail to activate early engagement and welcome them to their new tier.

## 2. Scope
Morning shift only, daily at 3:00 PM. The newly upgraded VVIP player list is updated daily at 2:00 PM by ATL or above.

## 3. Golden Rules
- Do not send until the upgraded VVIP list is confirmed by ATL or above. The list updates at 2:00 PM.
- Morning shift only; send at 3:00 PM.
- Use the New Players template only. Never a template from another campaign type.
- The goal is recognition, not pressure. No hard-sell, no urgency.

## 4. Messaging Principles
- Welcome and orientation: congratulate the player on the tier upgrade.
- Introduce the VIP CS as the player's PIC.
- Explain what VIP support helps with: priority chat, dedicated assistance, fast issue resolution.
- Explain how to contact the VIP CS: Live Chat, Telegram.
- Optional: invite preferences (language, contact time, game preferences).
- Avoid overselling, hard-sell language, urgency. The goal is recognition, not pressure.

## 5. Guards
- If the list is empty or not updated by 2:00 PM, notify the ATL immediately. Do not send an unconfirmed list.
- Never send from an outdated or unconfirmed list.
- Double-check segment and brand before sending, to avoid mismatched welcomes.

## 6. Remarks
- Morning shift only, after ATL or above confirms the updated list.
- Always use the latest list, never an outdated one.
