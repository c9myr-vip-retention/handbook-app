## 1. Objective
Prepare and maintain the SMS player list and content in the Team Engagement File. SMS sending is performed by the Retention Team Admin; VIP CS is responsible for list preparation only.

## 2. Scope
VIP CS prepares the SMS player list in the Team Engagement File. Retention Team Admin retrieves and sends the SMS via the SMS platform. VIP CS is NOT responsible for the sending action.

## 3. Golden Rules
- VIP CS responsibility ends at list preparation. SMS sending is done by Retention Team Admin.
- SMS house rules: no em dashes, keep the Cari tag spacing exact, under 160 characters.
- Never send expired content. Verify the expiration date before saving.
- Correct segmentation matters. A mismatched segment approaches the wrong player and causes reporting errors.

## 4. Guards
- Never hand over expired content; verify the expiration date first.
- Verify segmentation before handover. A wrong segment means the wrong player and a reporting error.
- Remove duplicates before saving, to prevent double-counting.

## 5. Remarks
- VIP CS responsibility ENDS at list preparation. SMS sending is done by Retention Team Admin.
- SMS house rules (content standards): no em dashes, exact Cari tag spacing, under 160 characters.
