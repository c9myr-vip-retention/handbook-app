## 1. Objective
Identify the top 20 winning depositors and engage them with VIP care. No bonus issuance, engagement only.

## 2. Scope
Night shift, daily at 8:30 PM. Target the top 20 players with positive P&L (winning) only. Losing players are covered under Top PNL and LTV.

## 3. Golden Rules
- Target positive P&L only (player winning). Losing players are skipped, handled under Top PNL.
- No bonus messaging. No SMS. Channels: Internal Message + Push, sent simultaneously.
- Focus is VIP care, interaction and relationship, not sales.
- If fewer than 20 winners, fill down the ranking to 20.
- Assess each player: highly active, long-term contributor, any recent churn signs.

## 6. Guards
- Engagement only, no bonus. Losing players are excluded (handled under Top PNL and LTV) to avoid over-engagement.
- If a winning player raises a complaint during engagement, handle it via the Complaint Handling SOP, not Top Deposit logic.
