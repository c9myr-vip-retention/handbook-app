## 1. Objective
Send targeted Internal Mail to players via the Back Office, based on brand, player segment and campaign direction.

## 2. Scope
Applies whenever Internal Mail is sent, on both TC and Zyra back offices. Zyra requires all three language versions.

## 3. Golden Rules
- Verify campaign type and recipient scope before sending. Sending to the wrong group cannot be undone.
- Zyra: all three language versions (EN-MY, ZH-MY, MS-MY) must be filled before the message can be created.
- Paste subject and content exactly from the approved template. Do not modify without TL approval.
- Screenshot the confirmation screen immediately, as mandatory QA evidence.

## 6. Guards
- Sending to the wrong group cannot be undone; verify recipient scope before sending.
- Zyra will not create the message until all three language versions are filled.
- If a link or content error is found after sending, report it to your ATL or TL immediately.

## 7. Remarks
- Always screenshot immediately after sending, as mandatory QA evidence.
- Zyra requires EN-MY, ZH-MY and MS-MY on every message.
