## 1. Objective
Engage selected VIP players through Telegram using a structured, priority-based approach. This SOP has three parts: the operational SOP, the engagement scripts, and the soft-skill playbook.

## 2. Scope
Telegram is a supplementary engagement channel. It does not replace Internal Mail or Livechat. Never promise, imply or hint at any bonus on Telegram.

## 3. Golden Rules
- Never promise, imply or hint at any bonus on Telegram.
- Work strictly top down the priority order. Never approach a lower priority while higher-priority players are uncontacted.
- One approach message per session. Wait for a reply before following up. Repeated messages get you blocked.
- No VVIP or Priority Support language in Telegram drive content.
- "Available" status is a last resort only, after all other approaches are exhausted. Ask the ATL.

## 4. Approach Priority Order
Work strictly top down. Never approach a lower priority while higher-priority players are uncontacted.

| Priority | Segment | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Churn | inactive 30 days |
| 2 | Churn | inactive 14 days |
| 3 | Churn | inactive 7 days |
| 4 | Churn | inactive 3 days |
| 5 | Churn | inactive 1 day |
| 6 | Grace Period | within grace window |
| 7 | High P&L | big win or loss today |
| 8 | LTV | loyalty selection |

## 5. Engagement Scenarios
| Scenario | CS Objective |
|---|---|
| Risk Player | Get a reply, learn the reason for inactivity, fix one issue, record preferences. |
| Newly Upgraded VVIP | Establish the relationship, get consent for contact style, capture preferences, set expectation that VIP CS is useful. |
| High-Loss Player | Welfare check. Support options, reduce pressure, protect the relationship. Not to drive activity. |
| High Deposit / LTV | White-glove care, reinforce trust, improve service, without pushing bigger deposits. |

## 6. Timing Guardrails
- Newly Upgraded VVIP: soon after the 2:00 PM list update, in normal service hours.
- Risk Player: same day the inactivity tier is first detected.
- High-Loss Player: wait until at least the next day. Never message while emotions are high.
- High Deposit / LTV: weekly, or scheduled relationship check-ins.

## 7. Soft-Skill Playbook (Part C)
1. Understand the starting point. Promo-claim players think "no reason to stay". Shift that to "someone takes care of me here".
2. First touch: start a conversation, do not jump into another promo.
3. Branch on response. Won: celebrate, soft-guide. Lost: acknowledge, reframe, never "win it back". No response: soft re-engagement later, no spam.
4. Become a personal assistant: recommend games at their level, respond fast, remember preferences.
5. Introduce value slowly, not just bonuses.
6. Micro-engagement builds habit. Consistency beats intensity.
7. Tag and personalise: preferred games, win-loss reaction, activity timing, deposit behaviour.
8. Avoid: messaging only when there is a promo, copy-paste spam, pushing deposits early, ignoring replies.
9. The goal is not "get deposit". It is to make the player feel guided, valued and comfortable. Deposits follow.

## 8. Guards
- Follow the priority order strictly; do not skip to a lower priority while higher ones are uncontacted.
- One approach message per session; repeated messages get you blocked.
- Never promise or hint at a bonus on Telegram.
