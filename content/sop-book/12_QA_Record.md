## 1. Objective
Record documentary evidence of every completed outreach task, for accountability, quality assurance and shift tracking.

## 2. Scope
Applies after every outreach task, every shift. The QA Record must be updated immediately after each task, not accumulated to end of shift.

## 3. Golden Rules
- Update the QA Record immediately after each task, not at end of shift.
- Never submit an entry without the required BO screenshot.
- If a task was not done, record it as "Not Completed" with a brief reason. Never leave it blank.
- Do not modify the date or category of a submitted record. Ask the ATL for corrections.

## 5. Guards
- Never submit an entry without the required BO screenshot.
- Incomplete QA records break the Output Report; complete all before shift end.
- Do not modify a submitted record's date or category; route corrections through the ATL.

## 6. Remarks
- Update immediately after each task, not at end of shift.
- If a task was not done, record it as "Not Completed" with a brief reason.
