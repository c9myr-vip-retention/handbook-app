## 1. Objective
Run the reload campaign on a controlled, rotating roster. Components: Ang Bao (TC), Redeem Code (Zyra), and Deposit Challenge (under testing). Each has distinct tier and platform targeting.

## 2. Scope
The Ang Bao / Redeem Code reload runs daily by batch, following the "Reload - Angpao and Redeem code By Batch" roster in Lark. Each day belongs to one team and its brand group. Issuing outside your team's roster day is a process violation.

| Days | TC (Ang Bao) | Zyra (Redeem Code) |
|---|---|---|
| Monday to Wednesday | Tier 3 | Tier V |
| Thursday to Saturday | Tier V | Tier 3 |

- The roster rotates over 3 weeks, then repeats, so every team gets an equal share of Mondays, Fridays, Saturdays and Tier 3 / Tier V campaigns.
- Some days are reserved for other campaigns or rest days. Always follow the roster for the date, team, brands and redeem codes.

## 3. Golden Rules
- Follow the roster. Only the team on the roster runs the reload that day, on its listed brands.
- Ang Bao is TC only; Redeem Code is Zyra only. Never cross-issue.
- Ang Bao is randomized, RM 8 to RM 18, for both tiers. CS does not pick the amount.
- Deposit Challenge is under testing. Confirm with ATL or TL before any issuance.
- If a player's tier is unclear, verify in the BO before issuing any reward.

## 4. Component 1: Ang Bao (TC only)
| Type | Amount | Tier | Platform |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ang Bao | RM 8 to RM 18, randomized | Tier 3 and Tier V | TC |
- One range for both tiers. The Normal Ang Bao (RM 2 to 8) is no longer used.
- Claim ticket query: `Special Reload Bonus`.
- TC platform only. Not applicable on Zyra.
- On your team's roster day only.

## 5. Component 2: Redeem Code (Zyra only)
Replaced Free Spin on Zyra brands in September 2026.
- Zyra brands only. Not applicable on TC (TC uses Ang Bao).
- On your team's roster day only. The day's codes are listed per brand in the roster.
- One claim per player per code.
- The player must deposit RM 100 to activate the code.
- Codes follow each brand's schedule in the roster.
- If a player reports an Invalid or Expired code, follow the Redeem Code S0403 SOP before escalating.

## 7. Guards
- Issuing outside your team's roster day, or on brands not listed for that day, is a process violation.
- Ang Bao (TC only) and Redeem Code (Zyra only) are on different platforms; do not cross-issue.
- Deposit Challenge is under testing; confirm with ATL or TL before any issuance.
- Never share redeem codes outside the players targeted for that day.

## 8. Remarks
- Why the rotation: it spreads CS workload (one team per day), gives each team an equal share of high-traffic days, and alternates Tier 3 and Tier V between TC and Zyra to avoid player fatigue.
- Free Spin (Zyra: 20 spins Tier 3, 30 spins Tier V) was discontinued in September 2026. Do not issue Free Spin.
