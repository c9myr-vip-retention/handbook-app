## 1. Objective
The pre-shift setup steps required of all staff before clocking in and starting work.

## 2. Scope
Every staff member, before every shift. This is the department's operational pre-shift checklist. The full attendance policy is owned by HR (see the HR SOP Library, Chapter 7).

## 3. Golden Rules
- VPN must stay on for the entire shift. Report disconnections to the ATL or TL immediately.
- Clock in at least 5 minutes early, to avoid a late record from system lag.
- Always browse in Google Chrome Incognito mode, for security. The full rule and its enforcement live in the Incognito Browsing SOP (38).

## 4. Guards
- Failure to clock in during working hours, or clock out after, on Insightful triggers progressive warnings (see Work Policy).
- Late arrivals are subject to monetary deductions (see Work Policy, Annex A).
- A wrong VPN location or a dropped VPN mid-shift must be reported to the ATL/TL at once.
