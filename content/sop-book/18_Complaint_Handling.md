## 1. Objective
Handle common Livechat complaint scenarios with empathy and strategy. Combine emotional acknowledgement with a strategic response.

## 2. Scope
Applies to losing-player and free-credit complaint scenarios on Livechat. Always acknowledge the emotion before moving to a solution.

## 3. Golden Rules
- Always acknowledge the emotion before moving to a solution or redirection.
- Never promise a bonus you cannot deliver. If unsure, say "let me check", never a false commitment.
- A credit is only ever given through the Compensation SOP: first-level handling first, last-week P&L ladder at SW x 40%, one per week, ATL approval.
- For the formal compensation decision, use the Compensation SOP; for amounts and tier rules, the Bonus rules.

## 4. Complaint Scenarios and Responses
| Complaint | What you see in profile | Recommended response | Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Losing a lot | Significant losses this week, SW has balance, no recent credits | "Saya nampak boss memang rugi sikit hari ni. Saya faham perasaan boss. Bagi saya check dulu ye." | Do first-level handling (luck, Jackpot, activities). If the player keeps asking and stays upset, follow the Compensation SOP. Never promise the credit before the ATL approves. |
| Losing little | Small losses, not enough for SW credit | Step 1 acknowledge: "Boss faham tu, memang kadang rasa macam tak kena." Step 2 game suggestion. Step 3 reframe toward hope. | Validate the feeling without committing a credit. Offer a fresh game. Reframe losses as timing, not bad luck. |
| Always asks for free credit | Habitual requests, no qualifying condition | Decline gently: "Boss hari ni belum ada free credit lagi ye." Redirect to a game. Promise future notification by SMS. | Redirect without rejection. The player should leave feeling looked after even without getting what they asked. |

## 5. Building Relationships Through Complaint Moments
- Malaysian events and holidays: chat about festivities, long weekends, public holidays.
- Food and local culture: a universal opener. Ask about favourite dishes, hawker stalls, lunch.
- Game discussions: games they enjoy, new titles, jackpot stories, recent wins.
- Use complaint moments to listen and learn. Every complaint is data about the player's preferences and pain points.

## 6. Guards
- No false commitment: if you cannot confirm a credit, say "let me check" first.
- SW credit only when the scenario and remaining allowance permit.
- Emotion first, solution second; skipping the acknowledgement loses the relationship.
