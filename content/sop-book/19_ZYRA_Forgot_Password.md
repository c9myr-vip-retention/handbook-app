## 1. Objective
Restore access for a ZYRA member who has forgotten their password, by verifying identity in full and issuing a random reset password.

## 2. Scope
Applies when a ZYRA member cannot log in or has forgotten their password and asks for help.

## 3. Golden Rules
- Verify all four identity items before any reset: UID, registered name, phone number (BO shows the last 4 digits), registered bank name and bank account number.
- Perform a fresh verification each chat. Never rely on a previous verification.
- The random password is temporary; the member must set their own after login.

## 4. Pre-Reset Verification (mandatory)
Verify all of the following before any reset:
1. UID
2. Registered name
3. Phone number (the BO shows the last 4 digits for verification)
4. Registered bank name and bank account number
Make sure every item is verified correctly before proceeding. Perform a fresh verification each chat, never rely on a previous one.

## 5. Expected Outcome
The member regains access, sets a new password of their own, and can use the account normally.

## 6. Guards
- No reset without complete, fresh identity verification.
- Never keep or reuse the temporary password; the member must replace it at first login.

## 7. Remarks
- Verification is mandatory and must be complete before resetting.
- The random password is temporary; the member must set their own after login.
