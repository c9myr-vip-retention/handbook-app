## 1. Objective
Deliver a personalised VVIP experience by requiring every CS staff member to review the player's previous conversation history before engaging, across both LiveChat and Telegram.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all inbound and outbound VVIP contact, on every brand, in both directions of channel crossover.

## 3. Golden Rules
- Before replying to any VVIP player, review the Last Telegram Chat Link or Last LiveChat Link to understand the player's history.
- Review both LiveChat and Telegram history whenever both are available.
- Never ask a player to repeat information they already provided.

## 4. Channel Crossover
The key is channel crossover: a player rarely stays on one channel. Before replying, check the other channel too, so you have the full history.
**Scenario 1: Player arrives from LiveChat.** Before replying, open the player's Last Telegram Chat Link and review their Telegram history as well, so nothing they raised on Telegram is missed.
**Scenario 2: Player arrives from Telegram.** Before replying, open the player's Last LiveChat Link and review their LiveChat history as well, so nothing they raised on LiveChat is missed.
> The source doc illustrates each scenario with a screenshot guide showing where the Last Telegram Chat Link and Last LiveChat Link sit. Follow the on-screen guide for the exact click path.

## 5. CS Responsibilities
1. Always check conversation history before contacting or replying to a player.
2. Review both LiveChat and Telegram history whenever both are available.
3. Never ask a player to repeat information already provided.
4. Follow up on unresolved issues and pending requests.
5. Update the player record after every interaction.

## 6. Expected Outcome
- Better VVIP customer experience
- Consistent communication across all channels
- Reduced repetitive questioning
- Faster issue resolution
- Improved player satisfaction and retention
