## 1. Objective
Reward eligible Tier 3 and Tier V players on their birthday with a controlled credit, once per year, sized to the player's own value and never stacked with another reward.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all VIP CS on every brand. High-value cases (lifetime Total Deposit at or above RM 100,000) must be handled by the ATL or above. One claim per player per calendar year.

## 3. Golden Rules
- Has SW: deduct from SW, 1x turnover. No SW / deposit base: no SW deduction, 3x turnover.
- Lifetime Total Deposit at or above RM 100,000: ATL or above approval. CS cannot issue directly.
- One claim per player per calendar year.
- No guaranteed amount before the eligibility check.
- No stacking with any other reward.

## 4. Step 1: Pull the Birthday List
- Pull today's Tier 3 / Tier V birthday players.
- The birthday source must be available.
- Birthday Reward Claimed This Year must be No.

## 5. Step 2: Basic Screening
Check each player one by one. Any Yes means skip.

| Check | Yes | No |
|---|---|---|
| Already claimed the birthday credit this year | Skip | Continue |
| Abuse or fraud flag | Skip | Continue |
| Listed for another bonus (special reload, LTV or any other) | Skip | Continue |

The "at risk" tag (days since last deposit, for example 3 Days at risk) is not a screening reason.

## 6. Step 3 and 4A: Confirm the Base, SW Base Calculation
| Player state | Base | SW deduction | Turnover |
|---|---|---|---|
| Has Secret Wallet | SW base | Deduct from SW | 1x |
| No SW / SW positive base | Last Month Deposit base | None | 3x |

| Tier | Formula | Max | TO | SW deduction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 3 | SW balance x 25% | RM 888 | 1x | Yes |
| Tier V | SW balance x 30% | RM 1,688 | 1x | Yes |

Amount rule: the final birthday credit must be at or below the calculated SW amount. Match the highest eligible 8-ending amount within it. If above the Max, use the Max. Deduct the issued amount from SW.
Examples:
- Tier 3, SW 200: 200 x 25% = 50, final RM 48.
- Tier 3, SW 500: 500 x 25% = 125, final RM 118.
- Tier V, SW 400: 400 x 30% = 120, final RM 118.
- Tier V, SW 4,000: 4,000 x 30% = 1,200, final RM 1,198. (Corrected 30 Sep 2026, ATL-confirmed: the earlier RM 1,188 was a typo.)

## 7. Step 4B: Deposit Base Calculation (No SW)
| Tier | Formula | Floor | Max | TO | SW deduction |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 3 | Last Month Deposit x 0.1% | RM 38 | RM 188 | 3x | No |
| Tier V | Last Month Deposit x 0.1% | RM 68 | RM 388 | 3x | No |

Amount rule: the base is last month's deposit. Match the highest eligible amount that does not exceed the calculated amount. If the calculated amount is below the Floor, use the Floor. No SW deduction.
Examples (floor and "never above calculated" applied; ATL-confirmed 24 Sep 2026):
- Tier 3, deposit 10,000: 0.1% = RM 10, below the RM 38 floor, final RM 38.
- Tier 3, deposit 100,000: 0.1% = RM 100, final RM 98.
- Tier V, deposit 10,000: 0.1% = RM 10, below the RM 68 floor, final RM 68.
- Tier V, deposit 200,000: 0.1% = RM 200, final RM 198.

Note: the original source doc's examples (RM 18, RM 108, RM 38, RM 208) broke its own floor and cap rules. The ATL confirmed the table on 24 Sep 2026; the examples above replace them.

## 8. Step 5: ATL Approval Gate
If the player's lifetime Total Deposit is at or above RM 100,000:
- Must be handled by the ATL or above. CS cannot issue directly.
- The ATL confirms player value, claim history, abuse or fraud flag and the final credit amount.
- Tag as `Birthday Credit - ATL Reviewed`.

## 9. Step 6: Issue and Record
After issuing:
- Mark Birthday Credit Rewarded.
- Record: tier, calculation base, amount, TO, SW deduction (yes/no), issued by, issued time.
- Update Birthday Reward Claimed This Year = Yes.

## 10. Guards
- Any screening Yes means skip the player.
- High-value cases (lifetime Total Deposit of RM 100,000 or more) are never issued by CS directly.
- Deposit base: never below the Floor (Tier 3 RM 38, Tier V RM 68), never above the calculated amount except to reach the Floor, never above the Max.
- No stacking: skip a player listed for another bonus. No guaranteed amount before the eligibility check.

## 11. Remarks
- Deposit-base floor rule confirmed by the ATL on 24 Sep 2026 (table wins over the original source examples).
- SW must be updated the same day before any SW-base sizing.
- V1.3 (3 Oct 2026, ATL-confirmed): deposit base is last month's deposit; the approval gate uses the lifetime Total Deposit; screening skips on an abuse or fraud flag, not the "at risk" tag; Tier V SW 4,000 example corrected to RM 1,198.
