## 1. Objective
Get an issue to the right level fast, so juniors do not sit on problems and the ATL only receives what genuinely needs them.

## 2. Scope
Any operational issue, player case or system fault a CS cannot close alone.

## 3. Chain and Principle
PIC / Agent to Senior to ATL to Supervisor to Department Head. **Absorb at the lowest level that can resolve it.** Escalate up only when the level below genuinely cannot.

## 4. Golden Rules
- Solve at the lowest capable level; do not push up what you can close.
- Escalate with full context: UID/username, brand, amount, screenshots, what you tried.
- Role titles only, never staff or player names.
- Time-box every escalation; do not let it stall.
- An RG or risk flag beats every marketing rule: no marketing message, route to welfare.

## 5. Common Escalation Triggers
- Confirmed system fault (e.g. S0403 after all CS checks pass).
- Withdrawal reject / duplicate / invalid account.
- Game maintenance or missing fund.
- Bonus dispute above the SW cap, or any amount over the approval gate.
- Any RG or risk-tag case, which routes to welfare, not marketing.

## 6. Guards
- RG / risk flag overrides everything: no marketing, route to welfare.
- No names; full context on every escalation.

## 7. Remarks
This SOP is the behaviour behind the Senior KPI metric "Escalation Ownership" - absorbing junior issues correctly instead of passing them up.
