## 1. Objective
Make every reported number true, comparable and reproducible, so no report misleads and every figure can be traced back.

## 2. Scope
All department reporting: daily closing report, KPI scorecards, ROI table, shift output, and any figure sent upward.

## 3. Golden Rules (non-negotiable)
- **Weighted, never simple average.** Retention = sum of depositors / sum of population. Never average brand percentages.
- **Contact precedes deposit** for any retention or reactivation credit.
- **Same-day figures are provisional;** only post-close data is reportable.
- **Missing brand = dash, not zero.** Flag cross-day comparisons.
- **Never invent a field.** Write "[not visible]". A visible gap is fine; a confident wrong number is not.
- **Correct misleading figures before presenting,** and quantify the artifact if a gain is mostly a data effect.
- **Name concentration risk every time** (LPK777 around 45% of team deposit).
- **No em dashes** (SMS vendor encoding).

## 4. Guards
- Provisional vs post-close is stated, never blurred.
- Weighted method only; missing = dash; no fabrication; correct-before-present.

## 5. Remarks
This SOP protects every other report in the book. If a number cannot pass these rules, it does not go out.
