## 1. Objective
Bring inactive VIP players back with the right message at the right inactivity stage, and credit a reactivation only when a contact preceded the redeposit.

## 2. Scope
Inactive VIP players segmented by days since last deposit (DSLD). Excludes any RG or risk-tagged player, who routes to welfare, not marketing.

## 3. Golden Rules
- **Contact precedes deposit.** No reactivation credit otherwise.
- Work strictly by inactivity tier.
- RG / risk flag beats everything: route to welfare, no offer.
- One reactivation count per player per month; the most recent contact owns it.

## 4. Inactivity Tiers
| Tier | Window | Approach |
|---|---|---|
| Early win-back | DSLD 7+ | Gentle check-in |
| Win-back | DSLD 14+ | Stronger win-back offer |
| Deep win-back | DSLD 30+ | Deep win-back offer |

## 5. Guards
- Contact precedes deposit for every credit.
- RG / risk to welfare, never marketing.
- No double count; most recent contact owns it.

## 6. Remarks
- The idle-days weighting mirrors the Reactivation KPI metric; confirm the current weighting table against the KPI rubric before relying on it.
