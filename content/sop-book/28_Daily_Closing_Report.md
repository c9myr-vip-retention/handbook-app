## Procedure

| Step no. | Step name | Specific action | Operating standard | Tools / forms used | Points to note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wait for post-close data | Wait for the daily close; exclude same-day provisional figures. | Post-close data only | Dashboard | Same-day figures are provisional |
| 2 | Pull per-brand figures | Pull all brands; a missing brand is a dash. | All brands; missing = dash | BO / dashboard | A missing brand is not a zero |
| 3 | Compute team totals | Weighted: sum of depositors / sum of population. Never average percentages. | Weighted method | Sheet formulas | A simple average overstates retention |
| 4 | Assemble in brand order | Section 1 brand order (LPK, BM, HOT, RM, SK, ACE, JK); name the concentration risk. | Correct brand order; concentration named | Report template | Name LPK777 around 45% concentration every time |
| 5 | Flag artifacts and risks | Quantify any data-artifact gain; flag cross-day comparisons. | Artifacts quantified; cross-day flagged | Report notes | Correct a misleading figure before presenting |
| 6 | Review and submit | Totals reconcile against the scorecard ledger; no em dashes; submit to the closing file; ATL sign-off. | Reconciled; ATL signed; submitted | Closing file; Lark | If it does not reconcile, do not submit |

## 1. Objective
Produce the daily team closing report accurately and on time, from post-close data, so leadership reads one true picture of the day.

## 2. Scope
The end-of-day Team B closing report. Follows the Reporting & Data Integrity SOP for all method rules.

## 3. Golden Rules
- Post-close data only; same-day figures are provisional.
- Weighted average, never simple average.
- Missing brand = dash, not zero.
- Name concentration risk every time (LPK777 around 45% of team deposit).
- No em dashes; no invented numbers.

## 4. Guards
- Post-close only; weighted only; missing = dash; concentration risk named.
