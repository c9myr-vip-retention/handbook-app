## Procedure

| Step no. | Step name | Specific action | Operating standard | Tools / forms used | Points to note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Log open items | In the last 15 min of the shift, log open cases, pending tickets, uncontacted lists, escalations in flight, and next-shift priorities. | All open items captured in the Handover tab | Team Engagement File; Handover tab | Nothing open is left unlogged |
| 2 | Note follow-ups with deadlines | For each item, note UID/brand, status, what is needed, and by when. | Each item has status, action and a deadline | Handover tab | A vague item cannot be picked up |
| 3 | Hand over to incoming | At shift change, the incoming CS reads and acknowledges the handover. | Incoming has acknowledged before starting | Lark | No acknowledgement, no clean start |
| 4 | Incoming picks up | The incoming CS works the flagged items first and queries the outgoing CS on anything unclear. | Flagged items actioned first | Handover tab | Do not start fresh work over an open flagged item |
| 5 | Close the loop | On resolution, update the status and tag it done. | Status updated; item tagged done | QA Record | An unclosed item reappears next shift |

## 1. Objective
Pass a clean state between shifts so no case, ticket or list drops between the outgoing and incoming CS.

## 2. Scope
Every shift change.

## 3. Golden Rules
- Nothing open is left unlogged.
- Incoming must acknowledge the handover before starting.
- Role titles only, never staff or player names.

## 4. Guards
- No open item left unlogged; acknowledgement required before the incoming shift starts.
