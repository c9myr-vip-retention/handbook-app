## 1. Objective
Compensate a losing, complaining VIP player consistently and within limits, so the player feels looked after without over-issuing.

## 2. Scope
Tier 3 and Tier V players who come to VIP Livechat to complain about losses or to ask for a bonus. Day Shift and Night Shift, during duty hours. Losing players already covered by the Top 10 P&L and LTV plans are engaged through those plans.

## 3. Golden Rules
- Acknowledge the emotion before any solution.
- First-level handling comes first and gives no bonus.
- Compensation only for a negative last-week P&L, at or below SW x 40%.
- One compensation per player per week.
- The ATL approves every compensation before it is issued.
- Never promise a bonus you cannot deliver. If unsure, say "let me check".
- No stacking with a same-day reward.

Example: last week loss RM 8,000 (ladder RM 98), SW RM 200, so SW x 40% = RM 80. Issue RM 78, the highest row at or below the cap, after ATL approval.

## 5. Guards
- Not a losing player (last week P&L not negative): cannot be given.
- SW insufficient: cannot be given.
- Already compensated this week: cannot be given again.
- No ATL approval: do not issue.
