## 1. Objective
Capture real incidents into the Case Study Library so the team learns from cases, not only from procedure.

## 2. Scope
Any notable operational error, player abuse or exploit, or provider/system issue worth learning from.

## 3. Golden Rules
- Real incidents only. Never invent a case to fill a category.
- Role titles only. No staff or player names, no player IDs or UIDs.
- "What we learn" is the point of the row; keep it to what a reader should do differently.

## 4. Guards
- Real only; no names or UIDs; the lesson is the point.

## 5. Remarks
- Source reports stay credited to their original authors. The library is the summary, not a replacement.
