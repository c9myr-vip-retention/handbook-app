## 1. Objective
Ramp a new VIP CS from day one to independent daily execution, then through probation, using the SOP Book as the training material.

## 2. Scope
From day one to confirmation. The probation evaluation itself is owned by the JNR Probation Checklist.

## 3. Golden Rules
- Accounts ready before the first shift.
- The SOP Book is the teaching material, not ad-hoc verbal training.
- A Senior buddy is assigned.
- Evaluate against the probation checklist, not gut feel.

## 4. Guards
- No first shift without accounts ready; buddy assigned; evaluation by checklist.
