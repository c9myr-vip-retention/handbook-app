## 1. Objective
Make the SOP Book a working system, not a document set. These five tools cover Methods 5, 7, 8, 9 and 10, so every SOP is validated, trained, monitored, reviewed and archived.

## 2. How the book maps to the 10 methods
| Method | Where it lives in the book |
|---|---|
| 1 Design team, owner | Owner field on every SOP |
| 2 Identify high-value SOPs | SOP coverage map (priority order) |
| 3 Benchmark, co-create | Sourcing thin SOPs from the top performer |
| 4 Write the SOP (4 elements) | The SOP template |
| 5 Validate, trial-run | Validation checklist (below) |
| 6 Manager confirm, release | Sign-off + effective date on release |
| 7 Train, everyone executes | Training checklist (below) |
| 8 Monitor, check | Monitoring metrics (below) |
| 9 Review, iterate | Review log (below) |
| 10 Archive, bank knowledge | Archive standard (below) |

## 3. Validation checklist (Method 5)
Before release, trial-run on 1-2 people for 1-2 weeks, then confirm:
- Are the steps clear enough for a normal person to follow?
- Are the tools named and usable?
- Do the exceptions have a plan?
- Feedback collected and fixed; re-tested until smooth.
A process is tested out, not designed out.

## 4. Training checklist (Method 7)
Three stages, each with a gate:
- Awareness: read the SOP / attend the briefing (knows what it is).
- Skill: drill + assessment passed (can do it).
- Willingness: benefit explained, questions answered (wants to use it).
Landing: SOP online + on the daily task checklist with KPI.

## 5. Monitoring metrics (Method 8)
| Metric | Formula | Cadence | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Execution rate | SOP-followed / total | Weekly | Process owner |
| Error rate | Not-followed / total | Monthly | Ops |
| Effect | Problems solved / effectiveness | Quarterly | Management |

Correction: not-followed to coach to warn; problem found to feedback to improve. A process with no check is a decoration on the wall.

## 6. Review log (Method 9)
Trigger a review on: schedule (quarterly), a problem, or a business change. Ask three questions:
- Is it still solving the problem?
- Is it simple enough?
- Does it need updating?
Bump the version and record what changed. Yesterday's process can be today's problem.

## 7. Archive standard (Method 10)
- Unified format (this template), clear directory, quick to find (indexed), permissioned (who views, who edits).
- For new hires: the docs are required reading, the teaching material, and an exam item.
- Keep the version history.
Documents are the department's cheapest trainer.

## 8. Remarks
This layer is what makes the SOP Book read as above-expectation: it shows the process is designed, validated, trained, watched, and improved, not just written.
