## 1. Objective
Define when a new staff member becomes entitled to KPI-based rev share, performance bonus and KPI incentive. Entitlement is gated on passing probation, so no incentive is paid before the person is confirmed.

## 2. Scope
All new hires (Senior or Junior) during the joining and probation period, across Team B. Covers staff incentive entitlement only: rev share from KPI, performance bonus, and KPI incentive. It does NOT cover player bonuses (those follow the Bonus SOPs). The probation evaluation itself is owned by the JNR Probation Checklist; monthly scoring is owned by the KPI Scoring SOP (24).

## 3. Policy Change (current update, effective September 2026)
- **Old rule (inherited from other departments):** a new staff member became entitled to the extra incentive after 1 month of working.
- **New rule (this SOP, effective September 2026):** entitlement is gated on passing probation. Probation is typically 3 months, set by HR, and may be extended or end in termination depending on whether the staff member meets the standard.
- A new staff member on probation is not entitled to rev share, performance bonus or KPI incentive, even past the 1-month mark, until probation is officially passed.
- **Entitlement start:** on passing, entitlement runs from the confirmation date forward. It is not backdated to the probation period, since the staff member was not entitled during probation.

## 4. Golden Rules
- No rev share, performance bonus or KPI incentive during probation. Entitlement starts only when probation is officially passed.
- The gate is the probation-pass event, not a fixed calendar month.
- KPI may still be scored during probation, for coaching and evaluation. Scoring does not equal payout.
- Confirmation must be official (ATL decision per the JNR Probation Checklist) before any incentive is released.

## 5. Eligibility Gate
| Stage | Rev share (KPI) | Performance bonus | KPI incentive | KPI scored? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| During probation (typically 3 months) | Not entitled | Not entitled | Not entitled | Yes, evaluation and coaching only |
| Probation extended | Not entitled | Not entitled | Not entitled | Yes, evaluation and coaching only |
| Probation passed (confirmed) | Entitled, from confirmation date | Entitled, from confirmation date | Entitled, from confirmation date | Yes, counts for payout |
| Terminated in probation | Not entitled | Not entitled | Not entitled | n/a |

## 6. Guards
- No incentive released for any period before the confirmation date (subject to the backdating rule, once confirmed).
- Scoring during probation is for coaching and evaluation only; never auto-convert it to payout.
- Do not apply the old 1-month rule. The gate is probation-pass.

## 7. Remarks and Notes
- Entitlement on passing runs from the confirmation date forward, not backdated (Section 3).
- Effective September 2026. The new gate applies to every staff member on probation from that date, including those mid-probation at changeover.
- Staff who already became entitled under the old 1-month rule before September 2026 are not clawed back. Confirm this transition treatment with HR / SPV if an edge case arises.
- Probation length (typically 3 months) and the pass / extend / terminate decision are owned by HR and the JNR Probation Checklist, not set in this SOP.
