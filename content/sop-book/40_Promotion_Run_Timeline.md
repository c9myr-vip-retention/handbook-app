# SOP: Promotion Run and Timeline

**Document Code:** C9MYR-RTN-SOP-B03
**Category:** Campaigns / Strategy
**Applies to:** All VIP CS; Promo Owner and Announcer roles; ATL / Supervisor for approval
**Owner:** VIP RTN / ATL | **Review cycle:** Quarterly or on material change | **Version:** 1.0
**Last updated:** 28 September 2026 (source: ATL briefing-improvement discussion; FIFA planning model, format reference only)

---

## 1. Objective

Run every promotion on one fixed timeline, planned before it starts, so the whole team knows what to do and when. Each promotion has one owner who handles it end to end and one person who announces it. The aim is to stop the team figuring things out only after go-live.

## 2. Scope

Any promotion the team runs: weekend components, special events, seasonal or ad-hoc pushes. This SOP owns the timeline and the roles. It does not replace the build detail: Weekend Campaign (15) and Special Event Campaign (35) still own their specific bonus ladders, windows and creative; Bonus Issuance and Approval (23) owns issuance; Push Notification (34) owns the send. This SOP is the rhythm that wraps them.

## 3. Golden Rules

- **Plan before you start.** Nothing goes live that was not prepared and briefed on the timeline first.
- **One Promo Owner per promotion.** One person handles the whole promotion: prepare, brief, follow up, review. Not shared, not scattered.
- **One Announcer.** One person makes the go-live announcement. A single announcement point, so the team gets one clear signal, not many.
- **No promotion goes live without approval and a hard end date.** Approval gate applies before any build (per SOP 35).
- **Contact precedes deposit** for any credit inside the promotion.
- **RG / chase flag beats the promotion.** Flagged players get no promotion message; route to welfare.
- Role titles only. No em dashes. State validity and turnover on every offer.

## 4. Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Promo Owner | Handles the whole promotion end to end: prepares the plan, gets approval, briefs the team, tracks follow-up, runs the review. One person per promotion. |
| Announcer | Makes the single go-live announcement to the team and, where applicable, sets the promotion live. One person. |
| VIP CS | Execute their part: send per channel, contact before deposit, tag, log QA. |
| ATL / Supervisor | Approve the plan and budget before any build. |

## 5. Promotion Timeline (the six phases)

The lead times below are **proposed defaults; confirm and lock them with the ATL**. Read T-0 as the promotion start day.

| Phase | When (proposed) | Who | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Prepare | T-7 to T-5 | Promo Owner | Draft the plan: window (hard start and end), brands, eligible tiers, bonus structure, platform split, content scope. Get ATL / Supervisor approval before any build (approval gate). |
| 2. Brief the team | T-3 to T-2 | Promo Owner | Brief all CS: what the promotion is, who is eligible, what to send on which channel, and the timeline. Confirm everyone understands their part before go-live. |
| 3. Start (announce) | T-0 | Announcer | One announcement to the team that the promotion is live; banners and content set live per the plan. Single announcement point. |
| 4. CS actions | During the window | VIP CS | Send per channel (IM / Push / SMS / Telegram), contact before deposit, honour any weekday / weekend limit, tag per promotion, log every send in the QA Record. |
| 5. Follow up | Daily during | Promo Owner | Track take-up and ROI, chase pending, watch concentration and abuse, escalate issues to the ATL. |
| 6. Review | T+1 to T+3 after end | Promo Owner | Tear down: expire or pull banners, close tags. Short post-promotion review: result vs target, what to change next time. Feed lessons back. |

## 7. Guards

- No go-live without a prepared plan, approval and a briefed team.
- One Promo Owner and one Announcer per promotion; never split the ownership.
- Campaign banner never set to Never-Expires; it self-drops at window end.
- RG / chase flag: no promotion contact, route to welfare.
- Contact must precede deposit for any credit.
