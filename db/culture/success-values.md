Seven values for how we serve VIPs and each other. The five pillars are how we think; SUCCESS is how we serve.

![SUCCESS: Service, Unity, Commitment, Change, Ethical, Selflessness, Strive. People. Solve. Problems. Together.](/flowcharts/success-values.svg)

## S: Service

> [!info|Core line] Serve the VIP properly: understand the real issue, then solve it.

| It is not | It is |
|---|---|
| "Reply fast and close the case." | "Make sure the VIP's problem is actually handled." |

**Ask yourself:** a VIP writes "the game is not working". What do I do before I reply?

**Good answer:** ask which game, which device and what they see, check game access, then guide them step by step.

**Key behaviours:** listen first; check before answering; reply clearly; confirm it's solved.

## U: Unity

> [!info|Core line] Work together as one team toward the same result.

| It is not | It is |
|---|---|
| "That's not my VIP." | "How can we help the team solve this?" |

**Ask yourself:** a teammate is handling a hard VIP case and asks me to check a promotion rule for them. Do I help?

**Good answer:** yes. I check the rule and send it to them; they keep the case and the VIP.

**Key behaviours:** share information; support teammates and other departments; one team, one result.

## C: Commitment

> [!info|Core line] Take responsibility and follow through.

| It is not | It is |
|---|---|
| "I said I'd check, then forgot." | "I said I'd check, so I update the VIP." |

**Ask yourself:** I told a VIP "I'll check this for you". What happens next?

**Good answer:** I check, update the VIP by the time I promised, and keep following up until it reaches the right stage.

**Key behaviours:** keep your word; follow up; finish with quality.

## C: Change

> [!info|Core line] Adapt and improve when things change.

| It is not | It is |
|---|---|
| "The old way was better." | "Why did it change, and how do I adapt?" |

**Ask yourself:** push messages now run on automation, and my manual step is gone. What do I do?

**Good answer:** learn the new flow and stop the old manual step, so nothing is sent twice.

**Key behaviours:** understand the reason first; learn the new process; suggest improvements through your leader.

## E: Ethical

> [!info|Core line] Do the right thing and follow the correct process, even when nobody is watching.

| It is not | It is |
|---|---|
| "Give the VIP whatever they ask for." | "Help the VIP within the rules and the process." |

**Ask yourself:** a VIP asks me to add a reload after the campaign window closed, "just this once". Do I?

**Good answer:** no. I explain the window kindly and offer what the SOP allows, with the approval it requires.

**Key behaviours:** follow the SOP and its approvals; protect player information; no shortcuts.

## S: Selflessness

> [!info|Core line] Think beyond yourself and support the team.

| It is not | It is |
|---|---|
| "Ask someone else." | "How can I help solve this?" |

**Ask yourself:** a new joiner keeps asking me basic questions during a busy shift. What do I do?

**Good answer:** answer briefly, point them to the Handbook or Learn page, and offer to go through it with them after the rush.

**Key behaviours:** share what you know; help new joiners; think about the team result.

## S: Strive

> [!info|Core line] Keep pushing yourself to become better.

| It is not | It is |
|---|---|
| "I passed, that's enough." | "What can I do better next time?" |

**Ask yourself:** I handled my first difficult VIP case and it went okay. Am I done with it?

**Good answer:** not yet. I ask my Senior what could have been better and note it for next time.

**Key behaviours:** learn from feedback; improve your service quality; don't settle for the minimum.

## People. Solve. Problems. Together.

The values work together: no good Service without Ethical behaviour, no Unity while thinking only of yourself, no Strive without accepting Change.

Before you act on a hard case, ask: which SUCCESS value should guide me here?
