Privacy notice

C9MYR VIP Retention Handbook, an internal staff reference.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google we receive your name and email address. Nothing else from your Google account is requested.

Why

Only to check who you are, decide whether you may open the handbook, and show who last edited a page.

Records

Sign-ins are recorded by our access provider (Cloudflare) for security. Edits to handbook pages are recorded with the editor's email so changes can be reviewed or undone.

Sharing

We do not sell or share this information. It is used only to run the handbook.

Questions or removal

Ask your superior. They can arrange for your access, and your sign-in record, to be removed.
